Lyon

The Gastronomic Capital of France
Landmark: Fourvière Hill
Cuisine: Le Coq au Vin
🏛️ History Lyon was founded by the Romans in 43 BC and became one of the most important cities in ancient Gaul. It later developed into a major center for silk production during the Renaissance.
🎭 Culture Lyon is known as the cultural heart of France, with museums, theatres, and historical architecture. The city is famous for its "traboules," unique hidden passageways.
🍽️ Cuisine Lyon is called the gastronomic capital of France. Popular dishes include Coq au Vin and Quenelles, served in traditional bouchons.
📍 Attractions Key must-visit spots include the Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière, the cobblestone streets of Old Lyon, and the vast Parc de la Tête d'Or.

Nice

Jewel of the French Riviera
Landmark: Promenade des Anglais
Cuisine: Salade Niçoise
🏛️ History Nice was part of Italy until 1860, after which it became a part of France. Its strategic location made it an important Mediterranean trade center.
🎭 Culture Nice reflects a vibrant Mediterranean lifestyle with art, music, and festivals. It is world-famous for its connection to artists like Henri Matisse.
🍽️ Cuisine The cuisine is fresh and Mediterranean-based. Famous dishes include Salade Niçoise, Ratatouille, and street food like Socca.
📍 Attractions Famous sites include the Promenade des Anglais, the historic Old Town (Vieux Nice), and the Marc Chagall National Museum.

Marseille

The Mediterranean Gateway
Landmark: The Old Port
Cuisine: Bouillabaisse
🏛️ History Marseille is the oldest city in France, founded around 600 BC. It has always been a major port city connecting Europe and Africa.
🎭 Culture Marseille is known for its diverse multicultural population. The city blends traditional French culture with strong Mediterranean influences.
🍽️ Cuisine Famous for seafood dishes, especially Bouillabaisse. Olive oil, fresh herbs, and the daily catch are essential components.
📍 Attractions Key landmarks include the Old Port, the stunning Notre-Dame de la Garde, and the turquoise waters of Calanques National Park.

Bordeaux

City of Art and Elegance
Landmark: Place de la Bourse
Cuisine: Canelés
🏛️ History Bordeaux has a long history as a major trading port. Wealth from maritime commerce funded its beautiful limestone architecture.
🎭 Culture Bordeaux is a UNESCO World Heritage city, perfectly reflecting classical French culture and sophisticated urban design.
🍽️ Cuisine Bordeaux is world-famous for its historic vineyards. Local specialties include Canelé pastries and Entrecôte à la Bordelaise.
📍 Attractions Don't miss the Place de la Bourse, the Bordeaux Cathedral, and the futuristic Wine Museum (La Cité du Vin).
